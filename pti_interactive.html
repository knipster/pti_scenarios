Data

Starting ratings: one row per player. Columns id, pti, sigma; optional name and lastPlayed (date of their match before the history starts, for break days). If the sigma is an official "Start Sigma" it already includes time decay, so leave lastPlayed empty.

Matches: one row per match, oldest first. Columns date, player1id, player2id, player3id, player4id, score. Players 1 and 2 are a team; the score is from their side, sets separated by commas (quote it), e.g. "6-3, 4-6, 1-0". Dates as YYYY-MM-DD or M/D/YYYY.

Files can be dropped on the boxes below, chosen from disk, or loaded from URLs. URLs can also go in this page's address: pti_interactive.html?ratings=ratings.csv&matches=matches.csv. URLs on another site need that site to allow cross-origin requests.
